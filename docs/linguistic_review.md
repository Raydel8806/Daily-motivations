# Linguistic Review of the Quote Catalog

Review performed with AI assistance on October 14, 2026.

All 366 entries were read and compared in Spanish, English, and French:
1,098 texts, including February 29. Interface labels, displayed dates, and
attributed author names were also reviewed.

## Results

| Language | Entries reviewed | Entries with wording changes | Entries changed, including punctuation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Spanish | 366 | 133 | 366 |
| English | 366 | 147 | 276 |
| French | 366 | 170 | 293 |

222 dates received a wording change in at least one language. Author names were
cleaned up or corrected in 45 entries, covering extra spaces, stray punctuation,
and spelling errors.

Typographic changes, such as final punctuation, spacing, capitalization, and
apostrophes, are counted separately from wording changes. Not every quote
needed to be rewritten.

## Review Criteria

- Preserve the meaning and metaphors of the Spanish catalog while using
  idiomatic English and French.
- Correct translations that confused the subject, tense, quantity, or meaning
  of a word.
- Correct agreement, accents, incomplete constructions, and literal calques in
  Spanish.
- Use the formal French form of address, `vous`, consistently when addressing
  the reader, while retaining first-person statements by the author.
- Standardize final punctuation and typography. Use spaces before French
  double punctuation marks and percentage signs, and before Spanish percentage
  signs.
- Remove the visible HTML entity in the August 7 quote.
- Display Spanish dates in the form `29 de febrero` without changing the
  identifiers linking quotes to their calendar dates.
- Use conventional localized names, such as Spanish `Confucio`, English
  `Confucius`, and French `Aristote`, and translate proverb labels.

## Change Record and Checks

[The full record](linguistic_review.json) includes all 366 dates, the languages
reviewed, and the before-and-after text of every modified entry. Date identifiers
and catalog ordering are preserved. Original and translated passages in this
record remain in their respective languages so the audit trail stays accurate.

Software checks verify coverage of all 366 dates, nonempty catalog entries,
absence of HTML entities and duplicated final periods, proverb labels, language
switching, sharing text, and calendar localization. These tests establish
content integrity and application behavior; they do not independently certify
linguistic quality.

## Scope and Limitations

This is a complete AI review of the text, not certification by a human
translator or a historical audit of all 366 attributions. The quotes are
editorial versions of the existing catalog, rather than verified transcripts
of each author's original words.

The June 9 quote had ambiguous wording about possibility and probability. It
was clarified while preserving that distinction; its original wording was not
verified. Some absolute or debatable statements remain as part of the quotes,
rather than as independently verified claims.
